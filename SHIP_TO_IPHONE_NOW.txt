📱 SHIP POCKETTELLER TO YOUR IPHONE - QUICK GUIDE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: CONNECT IPHONE
-----------------------
1. Plug iPhone into Mac with USB cable
2. Unlock iPhone
3. If prompted "Trust This Computer?" → Tap "Trust"
4. Enter iPhone passcode

STEP 2: OPEN XCODE
-------------------
cd /Users/mr.adams/pockettellerxchanges/PocketTeller/ios/App
open App.xcworkspace

STEP 3: SELECT YOUR IPHONE
---------------------------
In Xcode top bar:
- Click device dropdown (currently shows simulator)
- Select your iPhone name from the list
  (e.g., "John's iPhone" or "iPhone")

STEP 4: CONFIGURE SIGNING (First Time Only)
--------------------------------------------
1. Click "App" project in left panel (blue icon)
2. Select "App" target
3. Click "Signing & Capabilities" tab
4. Check "Automatically manage signing"
5. Select your Team (Apple ID)
   - If no team: Click "Add Account" → Sign in with Apple ID
6. Wait for Xcode to create certificates

STEP 5: BUILD & RUN
--------------------
Press Cmd + R (or click Play button ▶️)

Xcode will:
✅ Build the app
✅ Sign it with your certificate
✅ Install on your iPhone
✅ Launch automatically

STEP 6: TRUST DEVELOPER (First Time Only)
------------------------------------------
If iPhone shows "Untrusted Developer":
1. On iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
2. Tap your Apple ID email
3. Tap "Trust [Your Email]"
4. Confirm
5. Launch PocketTeller from home screen

TROUBLESHOOTING:
----------------
"iPhone is busy" → Wait for device preparation
"Code signing error" → Enable automatic signing
"Untrusted Developer" → Trust in iPhone Settings
"Build failed" → Clean build (Cmd+Shift+K), try again
