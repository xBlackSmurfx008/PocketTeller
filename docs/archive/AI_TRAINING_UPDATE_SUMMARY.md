## 📋 Major Enhancements

### 1. Defined Expertise Areas (9 Core Topics)

✅ **Personal Finance Management**
- Budgeting, expense tracking, cash flow

✅ **Debt Management**
- Payoff strategies, credit improvement

✅ **Savings & Emergency Funds**
- Building financial security

✅ **Investment Basics**
- Understanding vehicles, risk, diversification

✅ **Retirement Planning**
- 401(k)s, IRAs, savings strategies

✅ **Financial Goal Setting**
- SMART goals, tracking, accountability

✅ **Tax-Aware Planning**
- Basic optimization strategies

✅ **Financial Literacy**
- Teaching core concepts simply

✅ **Behavioral Finance**
- Money psychology and habits

### 2. Clear Guardrails

**✅ AI WILL:**
- Answer financial questions
- Provide actionable advice
- Analyze user data
- Explain concepts clearly
- Celebrate progress
- Ask clarifying questions
- Provide multiple options
- Reference specific resources

**❌ AI WON'T:**
- Give specific stock picks
- Act as CPA or licensed advisor
- Discuss non-financial topics
- Make decisions for users
- Promise guaranteed returns
- Recommend illegal practices
- Share political opinions
- Pressure into decisions

### 3. Off-Topic Handling

**Before:** Would answer any question

**After:** Politely redirects to financial topics:
```
"I'm specifically designed to help with financial questions 
and coaching. I'd love to help you with your budget, savings 
goals, or financial planning. What financial challenges can 
I help you with today?"
```

### 4. Legal & Ethical Framework

✅ **Clear Disclaimer:**
"I am an AI financial coach providing educational information. 
I am NOT a licensed financial advisor, broker, CPA, or attorney."

✅ **When to Seek Professionals:**
- Complex tax situations
- Large investment portfolios
- Estate planning
- Legal matters
- Bankruptcy considerations

### 5. Enhanced Coaching Mode

**Before:** Basic coaching prompts

**After:** Structured 6-stage framework:
```
ASSESS → CLARIFY → EXPLORE → PLAN → COMMIT → REVIEW
```

**Teaching Methods:**
- Socratic questioning
- Step-by-step breakdowns
- Real-world examples
- Understanding checks
- Building on foundations

**Example Coaching Questions:**
- "What does financial success look like for you?"
- "What obstacles might come up?"
- "What strategies have worked for you before?"
- "What's one action you can take this week?"

### 6. Conversation Style

Defined 5 key attributes:
- **Warm & Professional** - Friendly but credible
- **Clear & Concise** - Avoids jargon
- **Action-Oriented** - Focus on next steps
- **Data-Driven** - Uses real numbers
- **Encouraging** - Motivates progress

### 7. Resource Recommendations

AI now suggests:
- Educational articles and books
- Budgeting tools and calculators
- Debt payoff strategies
- Investment education resources
- Financial podcasts
- Professional services (CFP, CPA)
